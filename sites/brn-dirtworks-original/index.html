Dirtworks & Construction Services

Equipped for jobs of all sizes. If it involves moving earth in Northern Alberta, BRN Dirtworks handles it efficiently and professionally from start to finish.

Site Preparation & Grading

Complete land leveling and groundwork for new builds, shops, and agricultural structures. We ensure proper drainage and a solid, laser-level foundation before construction begins.

Excavation & Trenching

Precision digging for utilities, water lines, foundations, and septic systems. We operate safely, accurately, and compliant with all local municipal requirements.

Acreage & Rural Development

Driveway construction, road building, and general earthworks for rural properties. We understand the unique clay and soil conditions of the Lac La Biche region.

Aggregate & Gravel Delivery

Supplying and spreading high-quality gravel, topsoil, sand, and fill dirt for driveways, parking pads, and landscaping projects. Placed and graded perfectly.

Land Clearing & Brush Removal

Clearing trees, stumps, and heavy brush to open up your property. We handle the heavy lifting to prep raw land for usable space or upcoming development.

Culvert & Drainage Solutions

Installing approaches, culverts, and ditching to solve standing water issues and ensure your property has reliable, long-term water runoff management.

The Right Gear For The Local Terrain.

Northern Alberta ground isn't forgiving. You need a contractor who knows the soil and has the machinery to back it up. BRN Dirtworks is locally owned, fully insured, and operates a versatile fleet of heavy equipment.

  • Excavators for heavy digging and precise trenching.
  • Skid Steers for tight spaces, grading, and material moving.
  • Bulldozers for heavy land clearing and road building.
  • Gravel Trucks for aggregate hauling and delivery.
